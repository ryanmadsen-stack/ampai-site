The problem

Every bid starts with a takeoff, usually at night after a day on the tools.

Hours to days

for each bid: counting every device on the print and pricing the materials and labor by hand.

One missed circuit

can wipe out the profit on a job, and you find out after you've won it.

One wrong order

and the whole crew waits on the job site. That costs real money.

What AmpAI does

A full takeoff and estimate, circuit by circuit.

01 · READ

Reads the whole plan

Upload a phone photo or a PDF print set. AmpAI finds every device, groups them into circuits, and gives each circuit its own color on the plan.

Photo or PDF
02 · CHECK

You check every circuit

Each circuit gets an explicit Looks right or Fix this. Nothing reaches a bid until a person has checked it.

Human review built in
03 · PRICE

Priced down to the wire run

Every run measured in feet, up, through the studs and over the ceiling. Materials, labor and cost for each circuit, with the code reference behind each choice.

Materials · Labor · Code notes
04 · BUY

One buy list for the whole job

Materials for the job rolled into a single list, priced store by store, so the right parts show up on site the first time.

Ready to order
Also included

Side Job Mode

Type a small job in plain English, like “add a GFCI outlet in the kitchen,” and get a materials list, a cost and code notes. No plan needed.

Early 2027. Pilot contractors are using it now and helping shape what ships.

No. AmpAI drafts the takeoff and the estimate, and you check every circuit with Looks right or Fix this before any price goes to a customer.

What kinds of plans can it read?

Phone photos and PDF print sets of electrical plans. Bring one of your own jobs to a demo and we'll run it.
